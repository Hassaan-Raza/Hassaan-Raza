## A few things I've built

**NanoCodeGPT** — GPT-style transformer built from scratch in PyTorch. No pretrained weights, no APIs. Trained on 8,000 Python functions. Train loss dropped from 10.9 → 0.13 in under an hour on a free Colab T4.

**MediScan** — Multimodal AI diagnostic suite. Upload an MRI or medical report, get a structured diagnosis, an AI-generated anatomical body map, and personalised rehab exercise videos from Veo 3.1.

**MoonPulse** — Real-time global intelligence dashboard with live weather, air quality, earthquake alerts, and a Gemini-powered AI layer that generates city briefings and travel safety scores.
